## Signing and records

Read the [versioned agreement](https://github.com/just-tavrn/.github/blob/main/agreements/cla-v2.md).
On a pull request, reply to the CLA bot with exactly:

> I have read the tavrn CLA version 2 and I hereby sign it.

One signature covers your subsequent contributions to the public repositories of just-tavrn under this
version. A material change requires a new version and a fresh signature; old signatures are not reused.
A DCO `Signed-off-by` line is not required.

The bot stores your GitHub username and account ID, signature date, comment ID, and originating pull
request and repository in a private signature repository, including its Git history. The public signature
comment and contribution remain on GitHub. We use the private record to verify contribution permissions
and retain it while the contributions are distributed and as necessary to establish the associated rights.
Access is limited to the maintainer and authorized maintainers administering contributions.

The controller and private contact address are listed in the [imprint](https://tavrn.org/legal/imprint).
The [privacy notice](https://tavrn.org/legal/privacy#contributor-agreements) explains the legal basis,
GitHub processing, retention and your rights, including objections and complaints. Providing a signature
is voluntary, but we cannot accept contributions requiring this agreement without it. Questions, bug
reports and reviews without incorporated material remain welcome without a signature.
